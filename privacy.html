Privacy

Updated October 2, 2026

Who we are

Pomlets™ is made by Traceline Studios LLC, an independent game studio in Ohio. Questions about this note go to hello@tracelinestudios.com.

What this website collects

Nothing, unless you join the waitlist. The site uses no cookies, analytics, ads, or trackers, and its fonts and images load from the site itself.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service (GitHub's privacy statement).

The waitlist

  • If you join, we collect your email address. It's stored with Kit, the email service we use, and Kit sends you one email to confirm it (Kit's privacy policy).
  • We use it only to tell you about Pomlets: the beta, the launch on Steam, and the occasional bit of big news.
  • We don't sell your address or share it with anyone else.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted completely, email hello@tracelinestudios.com.
  • The waitlist is for people 16 and older.

Changes
