Tiny pets that live on your desktop.

Pomlets™ wander along the bottom of your screen, nap when you step away, and keep you company while you work.

For Mac and Windows. Coming to Steam.

Meet Pip, Tumble, and Wick

Three starter Pomlets, each with a little quirk of its own.

Pip

A moss burrower who tidies things. Its leaf ears droop when it gets sleepy.

Tumble

A pebble roller who collects shiny things for the mailbox, and rolls instead of running.

Wick

A lantern moth who's afraid of the dark, so it keeps a light on for everyone.

They get curious about your cursor. Pet one, or pick it up and watch it dangle. They help, too: a reminder when you need one, a timer, and quiet company through a focus session.

When you step away, they go home to sleep.
