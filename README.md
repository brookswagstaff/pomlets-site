# pomlets.com

The website of Pomlets™, tiny desktop pets by Traceline Studios LLC. Static pages served by GitHub Pages.

- `index.html`: the landing page, with the waitlist form (it posts straight to Kit; no scripts)
- `privacy.html`: the privacy note
- `thanks.html`: where Kit sends people after they sign up
- `confirmed.html`: where Kit sends people after they tap the button in its confirmation email
- `assets/`: images rendered from the game's own art, the stylesheet, and self-hosted fonts (Fredoka and Nunito, SIL Open Font License)
- `CNAME`: the custom domain

The pet art is made in the game's pipeline (`burrow/pipeline/blender/render_promo.py`). Pomlets™ is a trademark of Traceline Studios LLC. © 2026 Traceline Studios LLC. The art and text are not licensed for reuse.
